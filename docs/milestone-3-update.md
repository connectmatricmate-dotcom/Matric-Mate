# Milestone 3 update

Hi Adnan,

Milestone 3 is complete on my end. Here is what landed.

## The AI tutor is live

A 24/7 tutor that answers in the student's own language and stays inside the FBISE syllabus for
their class. Around it:

- **Ask a question**, by typing or by photographing the question from a book or a paper.
- **Ask AI** from inside any notes section, so a student never has to leave what they are reading.
- **Weekly coach**, a short personal report on the week with their two weakest topics and three
  things to do next.
- **AI practice builder**, which generates fresh MCQs, flashcards, blanks and short questions from
  the student's own chapters.
- **Mock board paper**, a full FBISE-pattern paper with the board's real section weightings.
- **Answer marking**, where a student writes a short answer and gets marks plus honest feedback
  against the marking points.
- **Revision sheet**, a one-page summary of any chapter.

Usage is capped per day so the running cost stays predictable.
